## Ingredients

- 1 cup (207g) urad dal
- 2½ cup stock or water ½ cup extra
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 2 star anise
- 1 tsp cloves
- 1 tsp whole peppercorns
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp salt, to taste
- lemon juice, to taste

## Instructions

1. Wash and rinse the urad dal and dice the onion
2. Saute the whole spices in the ghee using the instant pot until fragrant, 2min
3. Add the diced onion and garlic and cook until translucent
4. Add the garam masala and turmeric powder, mix well
5. Add urad dal, stock, and salt
6. (Optional) Add spinach, peas, etc
7. Prepare and add pot-in-pot rice
8. Pressure cook for 5min, naturally release
9. Add lemon juice and more salt to taste
10. If it is too thick, mix in ½ cup water
