## Ingredients

- 450g Flour
- 9g Gluten
- 250g Warm Water
- 80g Coconut Oil
- 5g Salt

## Instructions

### Prep

1. Add the flour and gluten to a bowl and rub in the fat
2. Dissolve the salt in the warm water and gradually work it into the flour until it forms a cohesive ball
3. Knead for 5 min, until the dough is fairly smooth and not sticky
4. Divide the dough into 20 balls about 40g each
5. Cover and rest for 20 min to 2 hours

### Cook

1. Heat carbon steel pan over medium heat
2. Roll out a dough ball
3. Without oil, cook 10-20 sec on each side
4. Stack the tortillas under a clean kitchen towel to steam and stay warm
